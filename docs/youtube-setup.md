# Article videos on the VaartaNow YouTube channel (manual upload)

Editors have two options after recording a video (Live Studio, AI News Generator, Review Queue):

1. **Save & publish on VaartaNow**: "Attach video" / "Publish now" stores the recording on our server
   and the article plays it.
2. **YouTube**: keeps our server free of video files.
   1. Record, then **Download video**.
   2. Open YouTube Studio (VaartaNow channel) → Create → Upload, and copy the ready-made **Title**,
      **Description** (summary, link to the article, hashtags) and **Tags** from the YouTube panel.
      Category: News & Politics. Audience: "No, it's not made for kids". Visibility: Public.
   3. Copy the video link (Share → Copy), paste it in the panel and press **Save link**
      (Live Studio: **Publish with YouTube link**).
   The article's banner then shows the play button and plays the YouTube video inside the article.
   The YouTube thumbnail is used as the cover when there is no other image.

A YouTube link can also be added later to any draft in the Review Queue.
